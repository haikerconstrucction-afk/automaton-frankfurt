Discover the Stunning Chalk Cliffs of Rügen – A Complete Travel Guide

The chalk cliffs of Rügen, known as Kreidefelsen, are one of Germany's most breathtaking natural wonders. These dazzling white cliffs tower over the Baltic Sea, creating a dramatic coastline that has inspired artists and travelers for centuries.

How to Get to Rügen's Chalk Cliffs

Rügen island is well-connected by road and rail. The nearest major city is Stralsund, which has a bridge connecting to the island.

  • By car: Take the A20 motorway and B96 highway to Binz/Sassnitz
  • By train: Regular connections from Berlin/Stralsund to Sassnitz or Binz
  • Local transport: Buses run regularly from Sassnitz to Königsstuhl National Park

Best Time to Visit

May through September offers the best weather, but also more crowds. For photography, early morning or sunset provides magical light on the white cliffs. Spring and autumn are ideal for avoiding crowds while still enjoying pleasant temperatures.

Top 5 Highlights

  1. Königsstuhl (King's Chair): The most famous viewpoint with a 118m-high cliff top platform
  2. Victor's Viewpoint: Less crowded alternative with equally stunning vistas
  3. Chalk Beach Walk: Trek along the shore beneath the cliffs (check tides!)
  4. National Park Center: Interactive exhibits about the cliffs' geology and ecology
  5. Jasmund National Park: Explore the surrounding beech forests and coastline

Local Food to Try

Rügen's coastal location means excellent seafood:

  • Fishermen's sandwiches (Fischbrötchen) with fresh herring or salmon
  • Rügener Balsam-Sprotten – smoked sprats in a special local marinade
  • Rügener Kreidebier – unique chalk-filtered beer from local breweries
  • For dessert, try Rügener Sanddorn (sea buckthorn) products

Whether you're seeking dramatic landscapes, peaceful walks through ancient forests, or simply want to marvel at nature's artistry, Rügen's chalk cliffs won't disappoint.
